Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

In short: SimCDU does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. The app only communicates with your own PC, on your local network.

About the app

SimCDU is a mobile app that displays the FMC (CDU) screen and the autopilot (MCP) controls of your flight simulator. It works with the free SimCDU Bridge program installed on your Windows PC.

Data collected

None. SimCDU requires no account, contains no ads, analytics or tracking tools, and sends no data to the developer or to any third party.

Camera

The camera is only used to scan the QR code shown by SimCDU Bridge on your PC. The image is processed on your phone. It is neither saved nor sent anywhere. You can deny camera access and type the PC address manually.

Network

The app only exchanges data with SimCDU Bridge, on your local (Wi-Fi) network:

  • phone to PC: the FMC keys and autopilot commands you use;
  • PC to phone: the FMC screen image and a few flight values (altitude, speed, heading).

These exchanges stay between your phone and your PC. Nothing is sent over the Internet.

Data stored on the phone

Only the local address of your PC (for example ws://192.168.1.23:8765) is saved on your phone, for automatic reconnection. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Children

SimCDU is not specifically directed at children and does not collect any data, including from minors.

Changes
